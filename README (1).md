# K-Means Customer Segmentation - Retail Store

This repository implements a customer segmentation model using the **K-Means clustering algorithm** on the **Mall Customer Dataset**. By grouping customers based on their purchase history—specifically their **Annual Income** and **Spending Score**—the store can tailor marketing strategies, optimize promotions, and enhance overall customer engagement.

## 🧠 Methodology

### 1. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- **Age**: Ranging from 18 to 70 years, with a high concentration of young adults (20–35).
- **Annual Income**: Ranging from $15k to $137k, showing a normal-like distribution peaking around $50k–$80k.
- **Spending Score**: Standard retail rating from 1 to 100 based on purchase behavior, distributed uniformly with a large group centered around 50.

### 2. Feature Scaling
Since K-Means is a distance-based algorithm relying on Euclidean distances, features must be scaled to have a mean of 0 and standard deviation of 1. We applied `StandardScaler` to prevent larger scale values (like Annual Income) from dominating the distance calculations.

### 3. Hyperparameter Tuning (Optimal $K$)
We evaluated the model for $K \in [2, 10]$ using:
- **Elbow Method (WCSS)**: Calculates the Within-Cluster Sum of Squares. A distinct "elbow" point is observed at **$K = 5$**.
- **Silhouette Analysis**: Measures how similar an object is to its own cluster compared to other clusters. The silhouette score peaks at **$K = 5$** (Score $\approx 0.55$), confirming that 5 is the optimal number of segments.

## 🎯 Business Insights & Recommendations

### 💎 Segment 1: Premium (High Income, High Spending)
* **Characteristics**: Wealthy, active shoppers. Primarily young to middle-aged adults (average age 32.7).
* **Strategy**: Retain these customers through **loyalty programs**, exclusive preview sales, and personalized high-end product recommendations. Focus on premium services and convenience.

### ⚡ Segment 2: Impulsive (Low Income, High Spending)
* **Characteristics**: Young demographic (average age 25.3) who love to spend despite having lower incomes.
* **Strategy**: Leverage **social media marketing**, influencer partnerships, flash discounts, and trend-focused campaigns. Offer buy-now-pay-later (BNPL) schemes or student deals.

### ⚖️ Segment 3: Balanced (Average Income, Average Spending)
* **Characteristics**: The largest segment (40.5%). Steady, reliable shoppers with middle-aged profiles (average age 42.7).
* **Strategy**: Focus on **mass marketing campaigns**, subscription models, and general discount coupons. Keep them engaged with consistent, value-driven communication.

### 🛡️ Segment 4: Sparing (High Income, Low Spending)
* **Characteristics**: High income but highly conservative spenders. Dominated slightly by males (54.3%).
* **Strategy**: Launch targeted, **value-oriented luxury campaigns**. Emphasize the quality, long-term durability, and investment value of products rather than simple deals.

### 🛒 Segment 5: Budget-Conscious (Low Income, Low Spending)
* **Characteristics**: Older demographic (average age 45.2) with limited income and interest in spending.
* **Strategy**: Limit ad spend on this segment. When targeting, focus on **clearance sales**, deep discounts, multi-buy savings, and essential household utility items.
